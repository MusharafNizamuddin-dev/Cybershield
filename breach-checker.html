What counts as a "breach"?

A breach means a company or website you had an account with was hacked, and your information (email, password, or other details) ended up in a leaked dataset that's now circulating publicly or on hacker forums.

If your email shows up here, it doesn't mean you're hacked right now — but it means you should change the password for that specific site, and any other site where you reused the same password.
