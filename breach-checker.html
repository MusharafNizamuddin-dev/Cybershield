How does this stay private?
  1. Your phone turns the password into a fingerprint (a SHA-1 code).
  2. It sends only the first 5 characters of that code.
  3. The service sends back every leaked fingerprint that starts with those 5 characters.
  4. Your phone looks for a full match by itself.

The password check uses the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords service. If a password is not found, that does not prove it is safe.

What counts as a breach?

A breach means a company or website you had an account with was hacked, and your details (email, password or other data) ended up in a leaked dataset that circulates publicly or on hacker forums.

Finding your email here does not mean you are hacked right now. It means you should change the password on that site, and on any other site where you reused it.

Email checks use the free XposedOrNot public breach database. Password checks use the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords service. Results depend on their data and may not be complete.
